# Gryphon's Roost
<a href="https://danprince.itch.io/gryphons-roost"><img height="40" src="https://static.itch.io/images/badge-color.svg" /></a>

A tactical deckbuilder about managing a team of fanatics trying to cleanse the land from an invasion of gryphons.

Build a party by adding cards to your deck then trying to play them tactically to clear out each roost of gryphons you visit.

The game is heavily inspired by [Slay the Spire](https://store.steampowered.com/app/646570/Slay_the_Spire/) and [Darkest Dungeon](https://store.steampowered.com/app/262060/Darkest_Dungeon/) and owes much of it's mechanical and visual design to those games.
